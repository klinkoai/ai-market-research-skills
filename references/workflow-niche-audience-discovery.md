# Niche Audience Discovery

Use to find coherent, underserved subgroups inside a broad market.

- **Inputs:** broad category, geography, product context, known segments, desired level of specificity.
- **Run:** submit the broad opportunity with `match_submit`; poll with `match_get`; inspect returned circles and use focused `circle_knowledge` calls for differentiation and unmet needs.
- **Decide by:** segment coherence, distinct needs or language, underserved evidence, reachability, and commercial relevance.
- **Return:** prioritized niches, what makes each distinct, evidence gaps, and the smallest test.
- **Boundary:** do not label a demographic slice a niche without a shared situation, need, or behavior.
