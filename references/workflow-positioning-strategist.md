# Positioning Strategist

Use to turn audience evidence into a differentiated and testable market position.

- **Inputs:** offer, selected audience, alternatives, proof, constraints, and desired action.
- **Run:** use `circle_knowledge` for needs, language, alternatives, triggers, and gaps; use `persona_knowledge` for buyer objections and proof expectations.
- **Decide by:** audience recognition, relevance, difference, credibility, and action clarity.
- **Return:** audience frame, category, core claim, supporting proof, objections, non-fit audience, and messaging tests.
- **Boundary:** do not present generated language as proven conversion performance.
