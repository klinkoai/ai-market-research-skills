# Buyer Persona Builder

Use to model buying behavior for a selected audience, not to invent a fictional demographic profile.

- **Inputs:** selected `circle_id`, offer, buying context, geography, and decision stage.
- **Run:** use returned `persona_id` values and focused `persona_knowledge` queries; add `circle_knowledge` only for shared context.
- **Decide by:** job, trigger, consequence, alternative, decision criteria, objections, trusted proof, and channel behavior.
- **Return:** evidence-led persona, buying journey, messaging implications, and unresolved questions.
- **Boundary:** separate observed behavior from inferred traits; avoid unsupported age, income, or identity claims.
