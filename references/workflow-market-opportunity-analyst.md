# Market Opportunity Analyst

Use to rank markets, segments, or opportunity directions before committing resources.

- **Inputs:** product or capability, candidate opportunities, geography, stage, budget, and constraints.
- **Run:** call `match_submit` for the opportunity question; poll with `match_get`; deepen only the leading opportunity with `circle_knowledge` when needed.
- **Decide by:** need strength, urgency, gap, product fit, reachability, competition, and evidence quality.
- **Return:** ranked opportunity portfolio, leading rationale, risks, and validation sequence.
- **Boundary:** opportunity evidence is not a revenue forecast or proof of product-market fit.
