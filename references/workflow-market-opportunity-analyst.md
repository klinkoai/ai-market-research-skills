## What is Klinko Market Opportunity Analyst?

Klinko Market Opportunity Analyst is an AI market research skill for turning scattered market possibilities into a structured opportunity assessment. It helps teams compare audience demand, problem relevance, strategic fit, market gaps, and practical constraints, then produces a prioritized view of where further research, product discovery, or commercial validation should begin.

## What this skill helps you do

- Translate broad ideas into comparable market opportunities
- Assess demand, fit, gaps, and execution considerations
- Separate attractive hypotheses from weak assumptions
- Prioritize the opportunities that deserve validation

## Questions you can ask

- “Which market opportunity should we validate first?”
- “Rank these product directions by commercial potential.”
- “Where are the strongest gaps in this category?”
- “What audience and problem combination looks most promising?”

## Example research process

1. Describe the product, idea, audience, or market you want to understand.
2. Add relevant public context such as geography, category, goals, or constraints.
3. Ask a clear decision question rather than requesting generic research.
4. Review the structured answer and use follow-up questions to refine the decision.

## How a market opportunity is evaluated

Market Opportunity Analyst treats an opportunity as a specific combination of audience, unmet need, value exchange, reachability, and strategic fit. It does not treat a trend, large category, or high discussion volume as an opportunity by itself.

| Opportunity dimension | What the skill looks for | Common mistake |
|---|---|---|
| Need strength | A recurring problem with a meaningful consequence | Counting mentions without understanding context |
| Urgency | Triggers, deadlines, risk, or cost that can motivate action | Assuming stated interest creates immediate demand |
| Whitespace | Gaps in existing alternatives and workarounds | Equating competitor absence with opportunity |
| Product fit | A credible match between the need and the team's capability | Choosing a market the product cannot serve well |
| Reachability | Practical channels for research, acquisition, and support | Ignoring the cost of finding and converting users |
| Evidence quality | Support, contradictions, missing data, and confidence | Turning weak qualitative signals into a forecast |

The output is a ranked opportunity portfolio and a validation sequence. It is designed to improve resource allocation before a team commits a product cycle, campaign budget, or market-entry plan.

## Market Opportunity Analyst compared with TAM analysis

TAM analysis estimates the size of a defined market under explicit assumptions. Market Opportunity Analyst answers a different question: which direction currently deserves validation first? It can organize sizing evidence and useful proxies, but it does not manufacture a defensible market-size number from public conversation volume.

Audience Finder ranks candidate audience segments. Customer Pain Point Analyst determines which recurring problems create meaningful demand. Startup Idea Validator tests the assumptions behind one concept. Market Opportunity Analyst compares broader directions where the audience, problem, gap, and product approach can all differ.

## Evidence boundaries

Opportunity evidence is not a revenue forecast, investment recommendation, or proof of product-market fit. Public sources overrepresent some communities and rarely expose complete buying behavior, budgets, or retention. The skill therefore separates observed signals from inference, records disconfirming evidence, and recommends a small test for the uncertainty most likely to change the ranking.

## Market Opportunity Analyst FAQ

### What should I include as a candidate opportunity?

Describe the audience, problem, proposed value, geography, stage, and important constraints. Candidates should be concrete enough to compare; “healthcare” and “fintech” are categories, not yet well-defined opportunities.

### Can the skill compare product ideas and market segments together?

Yes, when each option is framed as an audience-problem-offer combination. The analysis should make different assumptions explicit instead of collapsing unlike options into one score.

### Does the top opportunity mean we should build immediately?

No. It means the direction has the strongest current case for the next validation step. The recommended test should address the most damaging uncertainty before a larger commitment.

### What is a useful next test?

A useful test creates observable evidence: recruitment response from a defined audience, behavior around a current workaround, a commitment to a pilot, or payment for a narrowly scoped offer. The test should be capable of reducing confidence as well as increasing it.
