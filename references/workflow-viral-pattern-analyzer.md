# Viral Pattern Analyzer

Use to identify repeatable content mechanisms without confusing surface similarity or platform noise for causation.

- **Inputs:** selected audience, platform, content set or question, time range, objective, and available controls.
- **Run:** use `circle_knowledge` for high-performing and contrasting content evidence, hooks, formats, emotions, timing, and audience response.
- **Decide by:** repeated mechanism, audience relevance, contrast with low performers, transferability, and confounders.
- **Return:** pattern hypotheses, supporting and counter evidence, variables to preserve, variables to change, and test plan.
- **Boundary:** do not promise virality, copy successful posts, or treat correlation as causation.
