# Creative Brief Generator

Use after audience and positioning decisions to create an execution-ready brief.

- **Inputs:** audience, objective, offer, positioning, proof, channel, format, constraints, and CTA.
- **Run:** retrieve only missing audience language, triggers, objections, and content patterns with `circle_knowledge` or `persona_knowledge`.
- **Decide by:** single objective, audience recognition, message hierarchy, credible proof, channel fit, and measurable response.
- **Return:** objective, audience, insight, proposition, proof, mandatory elements, exclusions, deliverables, CTA, and evaluation criteria.
- **Boundary:** a brief organizes evidence; it does not replace creative testing or claim guaranteed performance.
