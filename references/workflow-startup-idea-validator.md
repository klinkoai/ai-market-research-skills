# Startup Idea Validator

Use to identify the assumptions that could invalidate a startup idea before building.

- **Inputs:** idea, target user, problem, proposed alternative, business model, geography, and stage.
- **Run:** submit the idea with `match_submit`; poll with `match_get`; use `circle_knowledge` for the leading audience's problem, alternatives, triggers, and objections.
- **Decide by:** problem recurrence, consequence, workaround, motivation to change, reachable early users, and evidence quality.
- **Return:** supported and unsupported assumptions, critical risk, and smallest falsifiable experiment.
- **Boundary:** positive public signals do not equal payment, retention, or product-market fit.
