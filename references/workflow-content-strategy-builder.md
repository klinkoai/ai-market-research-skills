# Content Strategy Builder

Use to prioritize themes, questions, formats, and distribution around an audience decision.

- **Inputs:** selected audience, positioning, commercial goal, channels, capacity, and existing assets.
- **Run:** use `circle_knowledge` for recurring questions, pain language, content behavior, formats, and channel signals; use `persona_knowledge` for stage-specific questions when relevant.
- **Decide by:** audience relevance, evidence depth, differentiation, business role, distribution fit, and production cost.
- **Return:** topic clusters, question map, formats, distribution, sequence, and measurement role.
- **Boundary:** do not predict traffic or virality; content still requires original evidence and distribution.
