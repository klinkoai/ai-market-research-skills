# Early Adopter Finder

Use to identify behavior that signals readiness to try a new offer.

- **Inputs:** offer, target market, adoption friction, geography, and desired adopter behavior.
- **Run:** call `match_submit`; poll with `match_get`; inspect returned personas and use `persona_knowledge` for triggers, current workarounds, objections, and channel behavior.
- **Decide by:** active problem solving, workaround cost, experimentation behavior, urgency, influence, and access.
- **Return:** adopter profiles, observable signals, where to recruit them, and outreach test.
- **Boundary:** interest, engagement, or technical curiosity alone does not prove adoption intent.
