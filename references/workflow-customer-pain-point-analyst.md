# Customer Pain Point Analyst

Use to distinguish recurring unmet problems from complaints, preferences, and online noise.

- **Inputs:** selected audience, product category, context, geography, and desired decision.
- **Run:** use `circle_knowledge` for situations, consequences, workarounds, alternatives, frequency signals, and contradictory evidence; use `persona_knowledge` only for role-level differences.
- **Decide by:** recurrence, consequence, workaround effort, urgency, and ability to motivate change.
- **Return:** ranked pains, customer language, evidence, affected situations, and validation questions.
- **Boundary:** negative sentiment alone is not a commercially meaningful pain point.
